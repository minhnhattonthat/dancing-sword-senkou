# Glossary - Dancing Sword: Senkou (GBA, MTO 2003)

Binding for every translator and reviewer. Agents propose additions in reports; only the controller edits this file.
Lint: a row's EN must appear when its JP appears in the line (warning count); an `Avoid` spelling is an error.

## Rulings (pre-approved by the user, 2026-10-15)
- Honorifics: dropped, conveyed through register (様 -> "Lord"/"Lady"/"my lord" only where the relationship needs it).
  Name order: given name first (Kyoko Kusunoki).
- Romanisation: no official English release or earlier fan patch exists -> modified Hepburn for Japanese names
  (Kyoko, not Kyouko); katakana Western names in their natural Western spelling (Claire, Emilio).
  OVERRIDE (official): the game's own English staff roll spells the cast KYOKO KUSUNOKI, CLAIRE LUDENDORFF, MIE MIE,
  RIN REI, LUCIER - those spellings win (Mie Mie, Rin Rei, Lucier). Voice notes below still say Mimi/Rinrei/Luciel.
- Credits: English. Graphics text (title, character-select names, intro scroll, banners): translated.
- Abbreviations: none. Full names and terms; the engine was widened (VWF, auto-paging) instead.
- Register: light, playful action-game banter; keep each heroine's verbal tics in English form (see Style).
  Sound effects / grunts: plain English interjections ("Ngh!", "Hah!", "Graaah!").

## Style rules
- Punctuation map: ・・・ / … -> `...` ; ！！ -> `!!` (max two) ; ？？？ -> `?!` or `??`; 『』 -> double quotes "..." ;
  （ ） (inner thoughts) -> keep parentheses ( ... ) ; ～ (drawn-out) -> repeat letters or `~` ; ♪ ☆ are available glyphs, keep them.
- Plain ASCII only, plus the glyphs ♪ ☆ ♥. Straight quotes.
- Each original page is one utterance; the build wraps it to 160 px x 2 lines and adds pages, so do not pad or squeeze.
  Aim for <= about 60 characters per original page (one English page); longer is allowed when the meaning needs it.
- Voices:
  - Kyoko Kusunoki: proud samurai retainer, feminine-haughty ("...isn't it?", "How pathetic."), loyal to her lord (殿 -> "my lord").
  - Claire Ludendorff: noble, devout ("O God..."), prim and sharp-tongued, proud of the Ludendorff name; calls her mentor 先生 "Teacher".
  - Rinrei: grim avenger, terse and formal, devoted to Lord Koun; calls enemies 貴様 "you wretches"/"you".
  - Mimi: cheerful glutton with a childish verbal tic (のら / なのら, slurred ら for だ). Drop the tic itself; keep a
    bouncy, kiddy, food-obsessed voice ("I'm sooo hungry~!", "Yum yum!", "Whatcha been eating?").
  - Luciel: quiet, dazed, few words, searching for her little brother Emilio.
  - Emilio: Luciel's little brother (エミリオは私の弟よ), childlike (uses ボク -> plain "I").
  - The "YEAH♪" rapper demon (チェキラッチョ): DJ/rap patter, "Check it out, yo!"
