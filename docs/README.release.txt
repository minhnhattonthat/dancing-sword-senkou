What is translated
- All story, stage, boss and ending dialogue (every page of the 200 dialogue blocks), with a new
  variable-width English font; long lines continue on extra pages automatically.
- Opening text crawl, character-select names, the title-logo subtitle and the staff-roll title card.
- Menus, HUD, options and staff roll were already in English in the original game and are unchanged
  (including its original "Charactor Select" spelling).

Names follow the game's own English staff roll: Kyoko Kusunoki, Claire Ludendorff, Mie Mie, Rin Rei, Lucier.
Honorifics are dropped and conveyed through wording; names are given-name first.
